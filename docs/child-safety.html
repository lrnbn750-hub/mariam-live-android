BAK LIVE Child Safety Standards

Last updated: November 23, 2026

BAK LIVE has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), child sexual abuse material (CSAM), grooming, or any sexualization of minors.

1. Scope

BAK LIVE is a social communication application that includes user-generated content, messaging, and live voice rooms. These standards apply to all users and all content and interactions on the service.

2. Prohibited conduct

Users must not create, upload, request, share, promote, or distribute CSAM; sexually exploit or endanger a minor; groom, solicit, or sexually communicate with a minor; encourage trafficking or abuse; or use the service to identify, target, threaten, or coerce a child. Attempts to arrange abusive or sexual contact with a minor are prohibited.

3. Reporting concerns

Users can report suspected child-safety violations and other abusive behavior using the reporting tools available in BAK LIVE. When submitting a report, include relevant account details and a description of the concern. Do not download, copy, or redistribute suspected CSAM as evidence.

4. Review and enforcement

Reports are reviewed by the BAK LIVE moderation team. Depending on the circumstances, BAK LIVE may remove content, restrict features, suspend or permanently terminate accounts, preserve relevant information, and report suspected child sexual exploitation to the appropriate authorities or child-safety organizations where required by applicable law.

5. Prevention and cooperation

BAK LIVE prohibits attempts to evade moderation or retaliation against people who report safety concerns. We may cooperate with law-enforcement authorities and relevant child-protection organizations in accordance with applicable laws.

6. Contact and safety concerns

For urgent child-safety concerns, use the in-app reporting tools to notify the BAK LIVE moderation team. Provide enough information for the team to identify the relevant account or interaction. Do not send or share suspected illegal images or videos.

These standards apply to BAK LIVE and are intended to support a safer service for all users. BAK LIVE may update these standards as its safety processes evolve.
